## What runs well with AI, and what broke

- Larry (OpenClaw): an agent makes TikTok slideshows and sends them through the Postiz API;
  self-reported millions of views. https://postiz.com/blog/ai-agent-tiktok-views-postiz-api
- Postiz on draft mode (vendor, own accounts): four new accounts that direct-posted by API were
  shadowbanned within about a week; rule: drafts only under 30 days or 20 posts, a human taps
  publish, slow warm-up, posting-time jitter; real photos 2.3x the saves of AI slides; one hook
  for two weeks went flat; self-hosted scheduler broke (OAuth refresh, full disk).
  https://postiz.com/blog/automate-tiktok-slideshow-ai-agents-postiz
- What humans kept in the runs that worked: account connection, the publish tap on new
  accounts, a daily look at numbers, the skill files. Generic unattended AI posts went flat.

## Brand deals

- TikTok One: 18+, a launched country, Community Guidelines and Branded Content Policy; projects
  need 1,000+ followers. https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-creators-can-sign-up-for-tiktok-one
  Older guides say 10k followers; likely outdated [unverified].
- Micro creators (under 10k): free product or about 20-150 EUR a video; vendor guides, wide range
  [unverified]. https://influee.co/blog/tiktok-influencer-rates
- For a game and app dev, direct outreach to indie studios and tool makers fits better than
  marketplaces. Outreach that works: short, a named person, one concrete idea, 2-3 samples.
- Scams: fake brand deals take over creator accounts (malware "contracts", login links, 2FA asks).
  https://www.bitdefender.com/en-gb/blog/hotforsecurity/how-any-creator-or-influencer-tiktok-account-can-be-taken-over-using-fake-brand-deals ,
  https://www.netinfluencer.com/200k-youtube-creators-targeted-in-massive-brand-deal-scam-cloudsek/

## German law and money

- Hidden advertising is banned (UWG § 5a; BGH 9 Sep 2021, I ZR 90/20, 125/20, 126/20): money or a
  free product means "Werbung" or "Anzeige", visible at the start; the platform switch alone is not
  enough [unverified detail]. https://www.ihk.de/chemnitz/recht-und-steuern/rechtsinformationen/internetrecht/kennzeichnung-von-werbung-durch-influencer-5270264
- Free products count as income at market value; Gewerbe, invoices and § 19 UStG are yskills'
  call [unverified, check limits].
- Contract points: deliverables and dates, revisions, label duty, usage rights (paid use priced
  separately), exclusivity, payment and term, cancellation, ownership.
