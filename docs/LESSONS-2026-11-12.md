## Patterns behind the rows

- **The gate measured what was easy, not what the owner looks at.** Rows 1, 2. A rubric needs one row per style rule, not only per defect class.
- **Counters and context died with the thread.** Rows 4, 5. What limits a loop must live with the asset.
- **The coordinator spoke ahead of the facts.** Rows 6 to 9, 11. Each cost the owner a question; several cost his trust ("wenn ich deinem wort nicht trauen kann", 11-11 14:22).
- **The owner was used as a blocker queue.** Rows 10, 12. Ask once, with everything, after our own options are used.
- **Rules that never reach the reader do nothing.** Rows 15, 16: the playbook hook was silent for project threads, and the coordinator has no hooks at all.
