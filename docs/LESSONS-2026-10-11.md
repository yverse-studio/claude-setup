## Next game: Being a DIK-style visual novel

He wants this built next (10-11 19:45: "genuinely a good game, we will probably build one"). What carries over
from `docs/HINATA-ROAD.md` (company-xy PR 86):

- Shape: a free-roam hub (the office) with a phone that starts things, scenes as text over posed pictures advanced by tap, a small "+1" toast instead of numbers, a gallery of every scene seen. The genre's moral-alignment gating and hard blocks are left out: one road, bond and flags only.
- Road as a state machine: office, call, talk, scene select, scene with a pose switcher, finish, afterglow, back to office. Data shapes (`scenes.json`, `poses.json`) are reused for every character.
- Heat bar, never a number; finish offered at the bar's end and also after the last unlocked scene so the player is never stuck.
- Production: one set, one rig, poses as data, cloth baked once per figure (`docs/SCENE-KIT.md`). Pre-rendered stills do not scale to ten figures.
- Workflow that ships it: story is the truth and scenes are fitted to it; one scene shot through the gate before the rest; same poses for all characters, then variation by scene; the private side stays behind the age gate and public files stay clean.
