# yskills' taste

Read by the design team (`frontend-design`, `impeccable`, `design-critic`) before every design
direction and review, in every project. A project's own `design/DESIGN.md` wins where they
differ. Every time yskills likes, rejects or comments on a design, the lasting part goes here
(one line, dated, with the reference), in the next claude-setup PR.

## Rejected
- 2026-10-06: Kenney cube animals "not good"; flat SVG people "really bad drawn" (3D toon figurines or none); pixel chibis "not cute or cozy"; a money screen of KPI tiles (copy paper money things: till receipt, cash book).
- 2026-10-07: characters that walk through each other, slide without a walk cycle or move too fast; an office that reads as a dashboard; a rounded 30 € next to 28,80 € (every amount two decimals, one source).
- 2026-10: early work "looks very AI" (the list: `SLOP.md`).
- 2026-10-18: Igelgarten v2 "holy ai slop": neon glow on every sprite on near-black, a dark SaaS menu with stripe rows in a garden game.

## Liked
- 2026-10-06: plush look (long fur, button eyes, head seam, felt clothes) over villager and chibi proportions; every character visibly dressed for its job; Animal Crossing villager cuteness is the bar.
- 2026-10-07: characters like booth.pm/en/items/4511536 with real spring physics (three-vrm SpringBone); cozy pastel, golden hour, round soft 3D.
