# Garment fit: one fit per base, every character wears it

Home of the rules: claude-setup `docs/clothing-pipeline.md` (steps, numbers, names) and the fit gate in `operator/gate.md` (Pipeline gates). This skill is the order of work and the traps; it restates no number. Role: `Character TD` (claude-setup `operator/roles/character-td.md`).

## Order

1. **Reuse check.** `python clothes_dupe.py <url or id>` before a download (exit 1 = HAVE, use it); `python clothes_dupe.py` for the whole wardrobe. Then look for a gate-passed `clothes\CL_*` piece of that kind and refit it. Docs first: the character's `CH_<name>\README.md` and the shape-key inventory.
2. **Prefit** (`tools/laptop/prefit_garment.py`, pipeline steps 2 to 3a). `preview <spec.json> <item>` writes front and side PNGs; `build <spec.json> <item>` writes `fitted/<base>.blend`. The spec per item: `file`, `pieces`, `s` (uniform scale), `rotx`/`rotz`, `d`, `decimate`, `tight`, `offset`, `center`. Rough placement only: the fit finishes it.
3. **Fit** (`tools/laptop/fit_garment.py`), run headless on the base file with `-P fit_garment.py -- measure|fit <out.json> all|CL_<item> ...`. `measure` first (the before numbers), then `fit`. Per piece it transfers weights, pushes cloth out of the body at rest, bakes a follow key for every body key under the same name (`CH_*` keys included), lifts torso cloth out of hollows, pushes out what still pokes per key, and writes `Hide_CL_<item>` for skin fully under opaque cloth.
4. **Measure** every state: rest, each follow key at 1 alone, each slider extreme, every `BT_` preset, every `CH_` key (each heroine with her real values), idle and the big pose. Then `scripts/blender/clearance.py` of claude-setup for the done report. A `BT_` or `CH_` key on the body that the report did not measure is a failure, not a pass: the gate lists what was NOT measured.
5. **Dress** with `dress.py <character> CL_<item>` (appends, rebinds drivers). Back up first (`_backup\<file>_YYYY-MM-DD.blend`), md5 in the catalog.

## The traps

- **FIT_SW binding.** The fit adds `FIT_SUB` (Simple subdivision, before the Armature) and two `FIT_SW0/1` Shrinkwrap modifiers (Nearest Surface Point, Outside, offset 4 mm) after the Armature. They touch only vertices inside the body or nearer than the offset, so loose cloth stays loose. The target is set per character by `dress.py`; the fitted file stores it empty. A Shrinkwrap with no target does nothing and passes silently: check the target after dressing. Two passes, because a nearest-vertex pass crumpled the cloth. Public practice agrees on Outside or Above Surface with a small offset (2 to 5 mm); Surface Deform is the less accurate choice for follow keys.
- **Shrinkwrap leaves the offset inside folds** narrower than twice the offset (butt crease, groin); tight pieces clear better with corrected follow keys and the skin's own weights, Shrinkwrap only for loose pieces.
- **Foot_In_Shoe.** Footwear is fitted and measured with `Foot_In_Shoe = 1`; the character driver sets it while any shoe renders. Never copy it as a follow key. A driver with a dead variable lets the shoe pass at 0: run the driver dump check of the pipeline gates.
- **CH_ keys.** One `CH_<name>` per character on the base, regenerated by the rebuild, never hand-edited. Fit files that only had Rika and Saki made every other heroine clip 16 to 35 % (companyxy report 2026-11-12): bake them all, measure them all.
- **Hidden means not evaluated** (no pose, no keys). Unhide what you measure.
- **Residual poke-through** is also hidden with the `Hide_CL_<item>` mask on the body, not only pushed out.
- **Armature scale stays 1.** Add body keys in batches, then refit every garment in one go.
- **Patterns on Generated coordinates** move when the rest size changes: use UVs or object coordinates.
- **Moved or re-dressed means a new fit.** "No visual change" never waives clipping.

## Done report

Per piece the clearance row (smallest mm, share inside) for each state above, the inventory of `BT_` and `CH_` keys, the comparison sheet, `Skills used: garment-fit` and `Docs read:`. You hand the numbers to the gate; you do not judge the look.
