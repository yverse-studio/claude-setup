# Rapid Q&A, answers only (from BuzzFeed's "30 questions as quickly as possible" and yskills' own 10-answers cut)

Watched 2026-10-26 (cloud, plain yt-dlp worked on the public Facebook link; frames, timeline, local transcript).
A solo phone shoot that reads like a pro Q&A. For his personal account: his face, his humour, no designed
cards ([`../../social-team/xyskillsx-style.md`](../../../social-team/xyskillsx-style.md)). Not shot yet; yskills: "for the future".

## What the two sources do

**BuzzFeed UK, Tom Holland (6:17, 1080p).** 30 questions, about 12 s each. Plain light backdrop, one
person seated, clip mic, eyes on the camera. 55 cuts, but most of the length is answers in a row; the pace
comes from speech (217 words a minute) and silence trimmed out, not from effects. The question sits in a black rounded
pill at the bottom (white text) only while it is asked. 2-3 gags where the screen leaves him: a Survivor tribal photo
with his face on it, a tropical island for "desert island", a real photo of a watch. Opens on the first answer, no intro
beyond one line. Closes with a plug line. Keep the structure: easy open, 2-3 long stories as peaks, short ones between, a
"talent" bit that fails on camera, a blunt last answer.

**yskills' 10-answers cut (43 s, 576x1024).** Only the answers, never the questions. Hook caption "Q&A without the Q and it
gets progressively harder to guess the question" is the whole idea and it is good: it makes people guess in the comments.
Shot in a chair in his room, white tee, clip mic (right choice), one 39 s take with 3 cuts, single-word captions, one glitch
transition, -17.8 LUFS. Raise: low resolution and dim, wardrobe doors and clutter behind him, he moves toward the lens so
the frame changes, no rhythm structure (it is one flat run), answers start before the viewer is in on the joke.

## Verdict

- **Take:** answers-only is the twist that is his; BuzzFeed's polish comes from light, backdrop, mic, trimmed silence and a
  fixed rhythm. Do the shoot as a Q&A, cut the questions out, post part 2 with the questions revealed.
- **Keep:** his clip mic, his caption look, the self-roast answers.
- **Skip:** 30 questions on TikTok (6 min is a YouTube length), the BuzzFeed logo and celebrity-interview framing, stock or AI b-roll.

## The format

| Part | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Caption from frame 1: "Q&A without the Q. It gets harder to guess the question." Answer 1 starts in frame 1 and runs under the caption | 0-3 s |
| Answers 2-3 | Easy, funny alone, 2-3 s each | 3-8 s |
| Answers 4-8 | Rising weirdness, 2-4 s each; one 6-8 s story as the peak | 8-30 s |
| Answers 9-10 | The hardest to guess; the last one is the blunt one | 30-40 s |
| Loop | Cut ends on a word that works as the start of answer 1 | last 1 s |

- **Departure from the style recipe** (7-9 s, 0-5 cuts): this runs 35-45 s with a jump cut per answer, because the payoff is the comment loop (people guess the questions, then the reveal), which a 9 s clip cannot carry. Safe test first: an 8-10 s cut-down of the 3 best answers with the same hook caption; post the long one if it holds.
- Question overlays in part 2 are plain white bold text in the same caption look, not designed cards.
- Total 35-45 s, 10 answers. Stretch 20 answers (60 s, TikTok minimum for rewards) only if the answers are all good.
- Post 2 (next day, pinned in a comment of post 1): same clips, questions on screen, "you guessed X, the real question was...".
  Guess-the-question comments plus the reveal give the share and comment loop his best posts have.
- Full 30-question long cut for YouTube or a later project: same shoot, keep the questions, BuzzFeed black pill, 4-5 min.

## Shoot plan (solo, phone, free, 30 min)

**Setup**
- Phone on its back camera (sharper), 9:16, 4K 30 fps if the phone has it, lock exposure and focus on his face (tap and hold).
  Phone on a tripod or braced on a shelf at eye level, lens at his eyes, never held, never selfie arm.
- Light: sit facing a big window in daytime, window in front and a little to one side. Turn the room lamp off (mixed light looks cheap).
  On the shadow side a white sheet or a white poster on a chair as a fill. No light behind him that is brighter than his face.
- Backdrop: one plain wall, a metre of space behind him. Take the wardrobe and clutter out of frame. A plain colour top that is not
  the wall colour (a dark tee on a light wall; the white tee on the white wardrobe blends in).
- Sound: the clip mic he already has, clipped on the collar about 15 cm under the chin, cable hidden; a quiet room, fridge and windows off.
  Clap once at the start of each take so the cut can sync.
- Question reader: his laptop below the lens, questions in big type, one at a time (or a second phone). He reads in his head and answers out loud.

**Framing**: chest up, eyes on the upper third, a hand's width over the head, left and right empty. Keep the top 15 % and the bottom 20 % free of
anything important (captions go there). Stay in one spot; do not lean in and out.

**Performance**
- Coffee first and warm up with 3 throwaway questions. Fast, full sentences that are funny alone; one beat of face after the punchline, then stop.
- Two takes per question, then next. Do the 10 shorts first, then the long ones if energy is left.
- Answers must work without the question: say the thing, not "yes". Take the odd detail (the crepe line rant is exactly it).
- Real answers only, not a script; where he has no answer he roasts himself.

**Cut (editor, per the Raise table in the style profile)**
1. Jump cut every answer, cut to the first word, no gap. Silences over 0.3 s out.
2. Alternate 100 % and 115 % crop on each answer so it looks like two cameras; no zoom animation.
3. Captions: his plain white bold look, one short line at a time in the top band, spell-checked; the key word of each answer, not the whole sentence. No
   emoji, no cards.
4. At most 2 real-photo gags (his own photos or clips, never stock or AI): one in the middle, one on the last answer.
5. Sound: no bed by default (final.mp4 may carry one); preset real_talk from [`../../sound-kit.md`](../../sound-kit.md), at most 2 SFX on the punchline, -14 LUFS; two masters (final.mp4, final-nomusic.mp4); he adds the trending sound in the app.
6. Caption options (3, he picks), all one line: "Q&A without the Q", "Guess the questions, I'll wait", "My therapist said keep it short".
