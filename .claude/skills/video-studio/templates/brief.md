# Brief: <video>

Producer fills this before any script. Keep it to one page.

- **Goal:** (followers, visits, installs, a sale) and how we will know (views, watch time, link taps)
- **Viewer:** who scrolls past, in one sentence, and what they already know
- **One feeling or action:** the single thing they take away
- **Type:** organic post / ad (paid, labelled) / how-to
- **Platform and length:** TikTok 7-15 s organic, 15-30 s ad
- **Real footage available:** which build, which screens or places, any voice memo from yskills
- **Earlier videos:** what they got (views, average watch time) and what to do differently
- **Must not:** claims we cannot prove, mocked UI, anything from `learnings.md` marked as a fail
