# Pipeline: idea to post to numbers

The one home of the social order of work (yskills 2026-10-26: "update our social team to pro level
and how it would really run until posting"; the first full run was the Y-Land brag post, below).
`SKILL.md` has the loop and the posting rules, `../video-studio/SKILL.md` the film steps 1-9 that
step 5 to 9 here call, `docs/TEAMS.md` (Social, Film) the models. Every role reads
[`learnings.md`](learnings.md) and [`xyskillsx-style.md`](xyskillsx-style.md) (international
audience) before its step, because both arrived late in the first run and forced rework.

## The order

Sizes S and M skip rows marked (L); a daily-lane video skips 4 and 6 (`SKILL.md`, Daily lane).

| # | Step | Role (runs as, model) | Takes | Hands on | Pass bar |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Idea in | Social Lead (routine or thread; Sonnet) | yskills' line, the feed, a `Done:` line | `social/ideas.md` row: brand, pillar, format, account | filed, 👍 only |
| 2 | Plan | Social Lead (Opus for the weekly plan) | idea, style profile, last week's Analyst lines, the audience slots | brief: job of the post (follow, tap, visit), format, length, slot, who is on screen | one job; matches his recipe or says why not; slot free; at most 2 a day |

| 4 | Script | Writer (`video-studio`; Sonnet) | brief, `hooks.md` | `script.md`: 3 hooks, one picked, beats with times, on-screen caption | `video-studio` step 3 |
| 5 | Hook check | Hook Checker (fresh subagent; Sonnet) [`roles/hook-check.md`](roles/hook-check.md) | script, `hooks.md` | `hook.md`: PASS or FAIL with the line that failed | every line 4 of 5 |
| 6 | Board (L) | Director, Critic on the animatic (`video-studio` 4; Sonnet, Opus) | script | `storyboard.md`, `animatic.mp4` | Critic PASS animatic |

| 13 | Engage | Community (webhook, daily subagent; Sonnet) | comments, first 60 minutes | reply drafts; leads to the coordinator | nothing sent in his name without the switch |
| 14 | Measure | Analyst (subagent; Sonnet) | `social/posts.md`, Metricool | after 24 h and 7 d: views, shares, saves, follows against his median; repeat, drop, test | a lesson that holds twice goes to `learnings.md` |

Rules that make it a team and not one agent in a row (T1, `video-studio` hard rules):

- Each role is its own agent; the Lead briefs and decides, never writes, edits or grades.
- The Hook Checker and the Critic made nothing they grade. A fail goes back to the step that owns
  the line, never to yskills; one fix round, then the Lead picks: drop, or ship a weaker cut with the
  honest verdict in the one line to the coordinator.
- yskills sees a cut only after step 10, or labelled "unchecked" when he asked to see it early.
  The first run showed him a jittering carousel because the motion check came after his look.
- An "upload" names one file. If it can mean two files, the Publisher asks the coordinator one
  line before scheduling anything.
- Not added, because a real small team folds them in: a separate Planner (the Lead's step 2),
  Compliance (Critic floor plus Publisher preflight), a Thumbnail artist (the cover is frame 0, the
  Editor's), a Social-listening role (the Scout and Community cover it).

## Timing for a post that must be live at slot S

Brief 12 h before, script and hook check 8 h, cut 4 h, Critic and copy 2 h, approve 1 h, scheduled by
S minus 30 min. Anything late drops to the next slot; nothing skips the Critic to catch a slot.
