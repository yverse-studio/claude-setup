# @xyskillsx style profile

Who yskills is on TikTok and how the team makes his videos better without losing him. Every role
that touches his personal account reads this first. Source: 30 of his own videos watched on his
laptop on 2026-10-26 (the 25 newest and the 5 most viewed, frames, timeline and comments), and his
words that day: "thats lowk the style i wanted but everything lacks quality cause i dont have time
... i dont want ai slop i want my style improved better but still authentic".

  2026-01-20, about 1.4k followers, 168k likes, median video about 400 views (2026-10-26).

## Keep (this is the authentic part)

- The caption meme: one short line, his voice, on screen from the first frame.
- His face or his body in the shot, and the joke on himself.
- Short (6-13 s) and loopable; the trending sound added by him in the app.
- Cooking with a joke or a real recipe in the caption (his Cooking Mama roots).
- Real moments from his phone, never stock, never a generated person or place.
- Replying to friends' comments with jokes; captions that make people tag someone or answer.

## Raise (where "no time" shows, fix these first)

| Low-effort sign seen | Better, still him |
|---|---|
| Monitor filmed with the phone (moiré, glare) | real screen recording, his face as a small cut-in or reaction |
| Dim light, shaky handheld | face a window, phone braced or on a ledge; stabilise and grade lightly in the edit (exposure, white balance, a touch of contrast, no filter look) |
| Caption over his face or under the TikTok UI | caption in the safe zone (top band, clear of the right rail and bottom bar), never over eyes or mouth |
| TikTok's default text, typos | the same plain white bold look he uses, set cleanly with a thin shadow; spell-check only copy we write, his own odd spellings and cut-offs stay (see Never); no fancy fonts, no emoji spam |
| "hold for 2x" or other burned-in reposts, filler "streak" photo posts | replace them with a real clip; every slot of his cadence (his call, `SKILL.md` Posting) gets a joke or a reveal |
| Talking head as one raw take | cut pauses and fillers, punch-in on the key word, captions in his words, under 20 s |
| Builder content as a polished ad (Y-Land cards) | builder content as a meme: his reaction plus the real thing working, ironic caption ("me after 6 h of coding a game nobody asked for") |

## Never (reads as AI slop or not him)

- Designed cards, gradient titles, kinetic-type templates, robot voice, generated faces or b-roll.
- Corporate or salesy captions, hashtag walls, "link in bio" energy.
- Over-editing: zoom on every word, whoosh on every cut, colour looks he would not use.
- Changing his humour into "content": the joke and the caption are his; offer alternatives, he picks.
- "Fixing" odd spellings and cut-offs in his own captions (yskills 2026-10-26: CREPEWITHMARZIPAN was his old name, STRAWBE is cut off on purpose, "you should understand those nuance"): they are deliberate jokes. Ask before treating one as an error, never correct it, and never write copy that claims not to understand it.

**Language and caption rules (all posts):**
- English only: caption, on-screen text, spoken words and auto-captions, no German word without a
  joke that explains it ("Spaetzle", not "Spätzle" in a hashtag; umlauts and ß only inside a quoted
  word).
- Plain global Gen Z English, short, one idea; no German slang, idioms translated word for word, or
  German brands and places the world does not know (Lidl, Bahn, Rewe) unless the clip is the joke.
- Dates "Oct 26", times "6 PM", money in $ or no number, miles or "km" with the number, Fahrenheit
  and Celsius both or neither.
- Hashtags: 3-5, English, one broad (#fyp is fine), two for the topic (#friedrice #hanoi), none
  German (never #fürdich, #deutschland, #germany unless the video is in Germany). No other
  language's hashtag walls.
- Trends: only sounds and formats that run in English-speaking feeds; check the sound page shows
  global use before he adds it; no Germany-only meme or challenge.
- Settings (his hands, one time): app language English, English added under Content preferences
  languages, location tag on a post only when the place is real and well known (Hanoi, London),
  otherwise none. No VPN and no faked region: it looks like bot behaviour and risks the account.
- The friends who comment from Germany stay: they are the core of the community, he answers them
  like everyone else.

**Community (authentic, not growth-hacked):** he replies himself, first 60 minutes where he can
(slot A), in English; one pinned comment that asks a real question; no engagement pods, no bought
followers, no follow-for-follow. Money stays parked until trust (project memory); when it comes,
the account's territory split decides which affiliate and brand lanes fit.

## How the team uses this

- Writer: write 3 caption options in his voice for every clip (ironic, short, one line); he picks.
- Editor: the "Raise" table is the checklist; the finished video should look like his best phone
  clip on a good day, not like an edit.
- Critic: score against the 5 hits above, not against an agency reel. "Would his friends notice it
  was edited by someone else?" must be no.
- Editor and Critic: an odd spelling or cut-off in his captions goes to yskills as a question, never as a defect.
- Sound and cuts: preset real_talk in [`../video-studio/sound-kit.md`](../video-studio/sound-kit.md) (no music, at most 2 SFX on the punchline, -14 LUFS).
- Delivery: `SKILL.md`, Posting.
- Re-check this profile after 30 new posts or when a video beats 50k; add the reason as a dated line
  in `learnings.md`.
