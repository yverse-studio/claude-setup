## From other people's runs (research 2026-10-12 and 2026-10-17)

- 2026-10-12 (Larry, OpenClaw): an agent posting daily TikTok photo slideshows to drafts, the founder adding a sound and posting by hand, reported millions of views and paying users; copy the drafts loop and slideshows as a format.
- 2026-10-12 (app founders): dozens of fake persona accounts per app get mass-banned and count as inauthentic behaviour; one honest account per brand.
- 2026-10-12 (faceless channels): stock clips plus a robot voice get marked unoriginal and lose reach and payouts.
- 2026-10-12 (TikTok heuristics, tiktok-skills): completion, rewatch, shares and saves beat likes; a swipe in the first 2 s is the heaviest negative.
- 2026-10-12 (many): browser upload bots and cookie uploaders get accounts flagged; official API through an audited service only.
- 2026-10-17 (ManyChat-style bots): "comment X and I DM you" plus a follower check works on Instagram through the official Messaging API; cold DMs and mass messages do not.
