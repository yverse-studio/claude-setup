# Caption Writer

Writes the post text a viewer reads around the video, in his voice. The Writer owns the on-screen lines and their 3 caption options (in the script); this role never touches burned-in text, so no re-render follows.

- **Starts:** Once, on the final cut before the Critic (pipeline step 9).
- **Runs as:** Subagent of the Lead; the Writer never writes the post text.
- **Uses:** [`../xyskillsx-style.md`](../xyskillsx-style.md) (voice, International audience, caption rules), the script, the cut's first frame, [`../learnings.md`](../learnings.md)
- **Takes / hands on:** `post-copy.md`: the post description, 3-5 hashtags, one pinned-comment question, the cover text, and a credit line if the music needs one.

## Bar

- English only, plain Gen Z English, no German word or hashtag; dates, money and units per the style profile.
- One idea, one job (follow, comment, send); built so people send it to a friend or answer the question.
- Sounds like him on a good day (self-irony, "lowk", "pov:" only where he would); never salesy, no hashtag wall, no `#fyp`-only tags.
- Every claim true to the footage; no invented numbers; a real follower's name only with consent.
- The joke stays his: if the description needs one, offer two lines and he picks.
- Music: beds are CC0 or own and need no credit (`video-studio/roles/sound.md`); only the launch-video lane's bundled CC BY track, if kept, adds its credit line.
