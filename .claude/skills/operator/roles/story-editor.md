# Story Editor

The critic of the Story team: scores a chapter against the fixed rubric (three runs, averaged), checks Sie/du and who-knows-what continuity, and sends failing beat ids back to the writer. Made nothing it grades. Team and rubric: [`07-story-team.md`](../../storyteller/references/07-story-team.md), `storyteller` skill (`05`).

- **Starts:** After a Story Writer's draft, as a fresh thread per chapter; a gate role, so its brief names `gate.md` and the kit's checks in `Prior art:` (`gate.md`).
- **Runs as:** A thread `Story Editor · <chapter>`, model from its row in `docs/TEAMS.md`.
- **Uses:** the rubric in the `storyteller` skill (`05`), `docs/game-pipeline.md`, the game's lint and state files
- **Model and handoff:** the brief names both; the rule is [project-manager.md](project-manager.md#model-and-handoff-in-every-brief).

## Every time

- Three runs of the rubric, rows scored 0 to 5; a 3 or lower on any row sends the chapter back with the failing beat ids and the rows.
- Continuity pass: who knows what, flags set, first events once, times and places, Sie/du per relation, against the cast sheets and the state delta.
- Report to the coordinator as a gate verdict (pass or fail per row); the fix goes to the Story Writer thread, which is not this thread.
