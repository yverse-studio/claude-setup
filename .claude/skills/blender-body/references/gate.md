# Gate: how a round is graded

Runbook with paths: `patches/base-body/REVIEWER-HANDOFF.md` (top block). Script:
`python3 -I tools/review/review_mg.py characters/luna/proof/mgN`.

## Standing reviewer
One reviewer thread grades every round. It owns the best-round history and the scores
(mg8 4.5, mg9 4.0, mg10 4.5, mg11 4.5 (teardrop by his eye), mg12 to mg14 4.0). A fresh reviewer per
round forgets what regressed. Replace it only if it is stuck or off the rules.

## Order (the eye decides, numbers last)
1. Frame contract: ref and body at the same scale, **crown and sole registered** (ears, hats,
   ponytails masked out), nothing clipped, hands whole, no stray objects. Else FAIL unscored.
   Print the head share of body height on every sheet.
2. Bust at full resolution, front and side, by eye vs his reference and the best round. Teardrop,
   flat pole, dent or sinking apex = cap 4. Judge shapes at the gate's own same-size registration,
   not the builder's alignment overlay.
3. Butt standing and squat vs his curve picture. Low shelf or tall columns = cap 4.
4. Neck at 2x front and side: no ring, shelf, facets or tube.
5. Openings at 2x: real forms with their own keys, clean borders.
6. **Posed frames every round**: from below, sway, arm up plus head tilt. No tube neck, groin fold,
   knee pinch; bust root stays.
7. Every view: front, side, back, back 3/4, each checked after every adjustment.
8. Compare with the best round; a trait worse than the best round fails the round.
9. Numbers last (band tables, apex, bow, shelf break); a number never overrules his eye. mg11 passed
   the numbers with a teardrop bust.

## Score
5 = bust and butt round like the ref, neck clean, openings present, poses clean, outline within
20 mm, nothing worse than the last round. 4.5 = one small item. 4 = bust or butt fails roundness.
3.5 = two regions fail or a regression. Never score a regression above the round it regressed from.

## 5/5 needs two checks
The reviewer re-runs all views itself, then a fresh independent reviewer with the checklist
(outline within 1 % on front, back, side and 3/4; no missing inner line; close-ups ref | best | new)
agrees. Only then it goes to yskills. After a body hand-in: gate, verdict plus 3 to 4 sheets, then
start nothing new until he says go.
