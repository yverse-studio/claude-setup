# 03 · Pacing: slow burn to escalation, with the beat sheet

## The intimacy ladder (count the rungs, one per chapter in act 2)

Each rung is a scene, each scene is a chapter beat, never two rungs in one chapter before act 4. Order can shuffle by archetype, the spacing cannot.

1. **Noticed**: he does one specific thing nobody else did (returns her pen, remembers her coffee).
2. **Named**: first name, nickname, or the dropped "Herr/Frau" (the honorific-drop beat; in German it's the `Sie → du` switch, which is a real event in a company).
3. **The first text**: a work message that carries one non-work line.
4. **Owned scene**: her scene type (`02`) played straight, the comedic engine running.
5. **Touch with a reason**: umbrella, steadying her, hair out of the face, the hand that stays one second long.
6. **Seen**: he sees her off-duty self (home, hobby, the second language). She lets him.
7. **The rival sees it**: a second heroine registers the shift; jealousy tell fires.
8. **The hold**: a hug or hand held with no excuse. Private thought beat for both.
9. **The question not asked**: the near-confession, interrupted by the device or by her choice.
10. **Doubt**: she pulls back (Romancing the Beat phase 3), the reason true to her file.
11. **The honest line**: she or he says it plainly. No misunderstanding survives this scene.
12. **The kiss**, in her owned scene type revisited.
13. **The night** (route lock in the game). Written in full, beat style, no fade (STORY-BRIEF Ergänzung 2026-10-11).
14. **The morning after**: the new normal, the small habit that proves it (her toothbrush, his coffee order known).
15. **The pact or the choice** (all-stay vs chosen-one, decided in the endgame contract).

A rung skipped reads as cheap; a rung repeated reads as stalling. The Angel Next Door spends a whole volume between 5 and 8 and readers love it; Rent-A-Girlfriend held the confession past chapter 380 and readers rioted. Target for the company story: rungs 1 to 9 across chapters 1 to 8, rungs 10 to 14 in chapters 9 to 10 of a heroine's arc, rung 15 in the shared finale.

## The second ladder: the women with each other (all-stay stories only)

Runs beside the intimacy ladder, one rung per ensemble chapter or jealousy storylet, never faster than the romance it follows. **Harem rule (`01`, yskills 2026-10-27): the ladder starts early.** Rungs 1 to 5 play as friendly rivalry from the first chapters, with no hurt and nothing blocked; rung 4 (admission) and 5 (the rule) may land in the first third. Rung 7 (the pact on page, private side) still waits for its two routes, but nobody has to be talked into it.

1. **Notice**: she sees him with the other one. A playful jealousy tell fires (chibi cutaway allowed), never hurt.
2. **Measure**: the two women size each other up in a scene without him (the bar, the kitchen, the lift).
3. **Truce**: a shared problem at work makes them a team for one day.
4. **Admission**: one says out loud, to the other, what she wants from him. The other does not laugh.
5. **The rule**: they set a rule between themselves (Girlfriend Girlfriend's pact logic), usually unfair to him, played for comedy.
6. **Ally**: one covers for the other with him. Private thought beat: *she's not the enemy.*
7. **The pact**: on page, with him, the lead woman of the pair consenting for a reason true to her file. Private side.
8. **Family**: the finale's new normal, all of them, the morning after at the office, who sits where.

## Scene beat (inside a chapter)

Goal → obstacle → turn → cost. Every scene ends with the state changed: a rung climbed, a flag set, a secret moved one head further. The "cute" scenes are not filler: a cooking scene is rung 4 and a jealousy tell at the same time.

The game's beat format (chapter JSON) is short narrator beats of two to four sentences, one moment per beat, plus her lines and sounds; each beat stands alone because it later captions an animated scene (STORY-BRIEF Ergänzung). So a chapter = 60 to 120 beats; the ladder rung sits in the last third; choices (2 to 3 per chapter) sit at the turn.

**Fan-service ladder** (yskills 2026-10-18: anime-style, lots of bust focus, ass too): runs under rungs 1 to 9, one beat or more per chapter, played as comedy and tension, never as a rung by itself. Glances (his eyes drop, she notices, the blush or the tease); accidental touching (the lift jolt, the fall that lands his hand on her chest, the crush in a crowd, the steadying hand on her hip); clothing moments (the button that pops, the wet blouse, the cardigan slipping, the bent-over drawer, swimsuit and onsen). Each woman's version is in her private sheet (`docs/story-bible/private/<slug>.md` §6). Heat 0 to 1 in chapters 1 to 4, 2 in 5 to 7, 3 in 8 to 9. Present-day adults only: never inside a flashback or memory of school days.

Heat 3+ beats live on the private side behind the 18+ gate; public files stay clean (PR 80 rule). The slow burn is the product as much as the act is.

## Pacing numbers we commit to

- One new heroine per 2 chapters in act 2, none after chapter 8 of an arc.
- Confession (rung 11) never before chapter 8 of a 10-chapter arc.
- A misunderstanding lives at most 2 chapters.
- One owned scene per heroine before any rival shares it.
- Every chapter: one rung, one laugh, one private thought per present character, one state change.
